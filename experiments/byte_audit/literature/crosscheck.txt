Reading
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Within a family the axis is the native knob: eviction papers index by token
budget, quantization papers by bit width. The mismatch appears only where a
table sweeps a ratio across both families, and that is rare, because the two
families largely do not appear in the same table at all.

So the finding is not that the field miscompares routinely. It is that the
cross-family comparison is the one nobody grounds, and the measurements above
say why: a fixed-bit-width method has two or three delivered footprints, so a
ratio axis with more columns than that cannot be matched at every column.
